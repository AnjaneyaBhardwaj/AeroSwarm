## Findings

Hand-written (session 14, 2026-10-15); `scripts/critic_eval.py report` keeps this section.

1. **The numeric validators alone hard-flag only sign and reference errors** (3 of 15 bad cases, no false
   alarm). The fidelity gap to NeuralFoil adds a soft doubt on the wrong-branch and reference-scaled cases
   (8 of 15 with soft flags, still no false alarm on the 11 trustworthy cases).
2. **Seven bad cases are invisible to every numeric check:** the 40-panel solves (Cl 0.05–0.10 below the 160-panel
   solve, nothing out of range), the stale images (the numbers are a clean design's) and the surface kink (a 0.4% c bump:
   Cl within 0.03 of NeuralFoil). They are what the vision arm has to add. In the images the kink is a
   spike at x/c 0.3 and the stale Cp encloses far less area than the printed Cl implies; the coarse-panel
   Cp differs only by sparse points and a softer leading-edge peak (the hardest case).
3. **LLM arms (2026-10-15, `claude-sonnet-5` via AEROSWARM_MODEL, one pass each, $0.33 text / $0.48 vision):
   the images added nothing.** Hard recall: numeric 3/15, text 4/15 (one reference-scaled case), vision 3/15;
   soft recall 8/15 in all three; no false alarm in any arm. Both LLM arms reproduce the numeric verdicts
   (sign flip, drag reference) and add almost nothing beyond them.
4. **The vision Critic sees the defects and explains them away.** From the findings
   (`evals/critic_results_llm_vision.json`):
   - surface kink: "a small spike/kink near x/c 0.28–0.3 corresponding to bubble location" (severity info);
   - reference scaled (Cl, Cd × 0.6): "suction-side -Cp peak and plateau area markedly reduced vs parent ...
     matching the large Cl deficit", although the plotted Cp is the unscaled one and matches the parent;
   - stale images: "area between curves consistent" with the printed Cl, which it is not;
   - coarse panels: nothing noticed (as expected: the hardest case).
   It reads the plots as descriptions of the stated numbers, not as an independent check on them. One pass
   per arm on 26 cases: a small, single-sample result, but no case moved in vision's favour.
5. **What would catch these deterministically:** Cl integrated from the plotted Cp (and the surface data
   compared with the stated geometry) is a number, not a picture. A "Cp-integral consistency" validator would
   flag the reference-scaled and stale cases without an LLM; the kink needs a smoothness check on Cp.
