## Findings

Hand-written (session 16, 2026-10-15); `scripts/of_mesh_study.py report` keeps this section. Cl in the tables is
race-car Cl (negative = downforce; NACA 0012's upright lift appears as -1.09).

1. **At the project's operating point (witness, Re 3e5) the default mesh is converged within 0.3% in Cl and
   about 3% in Cd.** Every variant stays within 0.014 in Cl; doubling the cells around (240 to 480) changes Cl by
   0.004 and Cd by 2.8%; first cell 1e-5 to 4e-5 c and far field 50 to 150 c change Cl by < 0.003. So the
   Re 3e5 disagreement with XFoil (Cl -1.44 vs -1.82) is not a mesh effect.
2. **NACA 0012 at Re 6e6: cells around and first cell are converged (Cl within 0.0015, Cd within 2%); the far
   field matters for drag.** 50 c gives Cd +8.9%, 150 c -0.4% against 100 c: the default (100 c) is within 0.5%
   of the 150 c result.
3. **Two variants diverged (NACA 0012, Re 6e6): 160 cells around and growth 1.16.** Coarse surface spacing and
   fast wall-normal growth at a high Reynolds number; both are now recovery-ladder cases
   (docs/OPENFOAM_RECOVERY.md).
4. **Growth 1.08 ended quietly with a wrong answer** (Cl 0.96 with trailing-edge separation from x/c 0.87) after
   only 4.5 orders of U residual drop, short of the 5-order criterion. A run ending without a crash was reported as
   "converged" here; `solver.convergence_status` now calls it `not_converged` (residuals short, coefficients
   steady: the ladder's business) or `unsteady` (coefficients still moving: a design signal), so such a result
   reaches the recovery ladder or the CAD agent instead of the ledger as converged.
5. **Default kept:** 240 cells around, first cell 2e-5 c, growth 1.12, far field 100 c (about 31k cells, 2.5-3.5
   min per solve on one core).
